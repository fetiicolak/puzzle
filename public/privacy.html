Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 August 2026

Birlikte Puzzle is a site that turns your photos into jigsaw puzzles so you can solve them together with the people you love. This page explains what we store about you, why, and what we do with it.

What we store

InformationWhy
Your email addressSo you can sign in to your account
Your display nameSo the people you play with can recognise you
Your profile photo (optional)Same reason
Your year of birth (optional)To show your age on your profile
Your gender (optional)To show it on your profile
The photos you uploadTo turn them into puzzles and to let you pick up where you left off
Your puzzle progress and timeSo you can continue a puzzle you left unfinished
Your friendships and messagesFor the messaging feature
When you were last seen on the siteSo your friends can see you are “online now”

Year of birth and gender are not required; you can leave them blank or clear them later.

Who can see it

  • Your profile details and photo are visible only to people you have solved a puzzle with and people you are friends with. Other users of the site cannot see them.
  • The photos you upload are opened only to the people taking part in that puzzle. Photos are not kept at a publicly reachable address; access is granted through short-lived signed links.
  • If you save a puzzle for a special day, nobody but you can open it until that date arrives.
  • Your messages are seen only by you and the person you are writing with.

Where it is stored

Data is kept on Supabase infrastructure (European region). The site itself is hosted on GitHub Pages.

While you play together, video and piece movements are transferred directly between devices. On networks where a direct connection cannot be established, a relay server (TURN, Metered) carries the traffic. In that case the content stays encrypted, but connection metadata (who connected and when) passes through that provider.

Kept on your device

Your unfinished games are also stored in your browser so you can resume quickly. They are deleted when you sign out, so on a shared computer the next person cannot see them. If “Remember me” is ticked, only your email address is remembered — your password is never stored.

For how long

Your information stays until you delete it. When you delete a puzzle, its photo is removed from the server as well.

Your rights

You can change or clear the details on your profile at any time. To delete your account completely, use the “Delete my account” button in the Profile window; this permanently deletes your puzzles, photos, messages and account, and cannot be undone.

You can send requests to access, correct or delete your data — including requests under the Turkish data protection law (KVKK) and the GDPR — to the address below.

Cookies and tracking

We do not use advertising cookies and we do not run third-party analytics. Your browser only stores what is needed to keep you signed in and to remember your preferences.

Children

This service is not intended for children under 13.
